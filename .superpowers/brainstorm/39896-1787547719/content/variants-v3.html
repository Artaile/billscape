Notes on this version

  • GST Mode per price (Sale = Inclusive, Purchase = Exclusive, independently) matches how IppoBill's own screenshot showed it — sale price is usually MRP-style inclusive, purchase price from a supplier invoice is usually exclusive-then-taxed.
  • SKU marked optional since most shops rely on the barcode alone; typing a custom SKU is still there for those who want it.
  • Expiry stays optional per-variant, same reasoning as before — not every variant/product expires.
