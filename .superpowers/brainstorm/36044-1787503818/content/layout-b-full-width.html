Why this works

  • Item entry gets the FULL page width instead of ~65% — every field gets more breathing room, no cramped 6-column squeeze
  • Nothing is hidden — Category/Unit, Variants, Batches are always visible as clearly numbered, labeled sections (not a collapsed "More details" toggle)
  • Each optional section has a one-line "why would I need this" hint, so it reads as guidance, not clutter
  • Bill Summary becomes a persistent strip pinned to the bottom of the viewport (like a checkout bar) — always visible, always reachable, without stealing side space from data entry

Trade-off

  • On a very long items list, you scroll past items to reach Save — mitigated by making the summary bar sticky to the bottom of the screen (not just bottom of the page), so it's always visible while scrolling
