## BillScape Differentiators (Keep & Market)
- India GST-first (GSTIN, HSN, CGST/SGST/IGST) — competitor is UAE/VAT-first
- Onboarding wizard (competitor has none)
- PWA — no app store needed
- Shop color branding / white-label feel
- Coupon codes with validity dates & toggle
- P&L report built-in
